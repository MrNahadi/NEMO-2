Shipboard secondary steelwork such as lifting padeyes, machinery-space pipe
support brackets and roll-stabilizer fins must satisfy distinct structural,
vibration and hydrodynamic rules while remaining practical to cut and weld
from shipyard plate stock. This report presents \NEMO{}, a local desktop
design tool that sizes lifting padeyes to ASME BTH-1, pipe support brackets to
AISC 360-16 with machinery-space pipe-span vibration screening, and
roll-stabilizer fin internal structures to IACS UR S10 with independent
foil-load and cavitation models.

From a plain engineering description of the shipboard job, \NEMO{} searches
available yard plate thicknesses, sizes continuous dimensions with a bounded
Nelder-Mead search, rounds to buildable sizes and returns three designs at
100\%, 85\% and 70\% maximum utilisation. Each design is built as an exact
solid in build123d, meshed in Gmsh at three refinements and solved with
quadratic tetrahedra in scikit-fem with automatic six-degree-of-freedom force
and moment equilibrium checks. A local browser interface records every
engineering step and issues a traceable PDF calculation package alongside STEP
and DXF fabrication files.

Verification reproduces a padeye hand calculation within 0.002\%, exact solid
volumes to 0.000\%, Timoshenko cantilever deflection and bending stress within
0.8\% and 2.6\%, a plate-hole stress concentration within 0.9\%, a circular
pin-loaded lug benchmark within published photoelastic bounds, fin-stock beam
theory within 0.13\%, and NACA~0020 lift slope within 1.7\%, supported by 181
passing automated tests. For a 5~t engine-room lift, the lightest padeye
weighs 0.305~kg and is governed by its base weld at 98\% use, with a converged
pin-hole finite element peak of 222.7~MPa against a 355~MPa yield. For an
NPS~4 seawater line, the bracket uses only 25\% of its plate strength, while
engine-firing resonance limits pipe support spacing to 2.3~m. For a 1.4~m by
1.2~m patrol-boat stabilizer fin at 18~knots, the low-aspect-ratio foil model
yields 80.5~kN against 66.7~kN from IACS UR S10, and combined stock bending
and torsion governs the 457.5~kg design. Across all three marine details,
catalogue hardware steps, minimum fabrication sizes, machinery vibration and
foil hydrodynamics govern before nominal plate strength alone.
